DESCRIPTION
────────────────────────────────────────
Make animated SVGs for the web: draw or import your artwork, animate it on a keyframe timeline, and export one small animated SVG file that plays on any website.

SVGcinema is a complete animation editor that runs in its own window. Everything you need is in one place: drawing tools, a layers panel, an inspector for every property and a timeline with keyframes and easing.

DRAW OR BRING IN YOUR ARTWORK
Draw with the pen, pencil and shape tools, add text with Google Fonts or your own fonts, and place images. Or drop in an SVG from Figma, Illustrator or Inkscape: its layers, shapes, gradients and text come in ready to edit, and CSS animations come in as keyframes.

ANIMATE ANYTHING
Every property can be animated: position, scale, rotation, colours and gradients, strokes and dashes, opacity, filters and more. Morph one shape into another, send a layer along a motion path, draw lines on with trim paths, and shape the timing with easing presets or your own curves in the graph editor. Onion skin shows where things come from and where they go.

RIGS, BONES AND PINS
Build a rig with bones, pose it with FK or IK, set limits, and bend artwork with pins and mesh warp, even pins that follow bones.

ONE FILE THAT PLAYS ANYWHERE
Export one self-contained animated SVG, with a small player inside or as pure CSS. Choose when it starts: on load, on hover, on click, when scrolled into view, or linked to the page's scroll. Also export video (MP4, WebM), GIF and animated WebP, or a still image (PNG, JPEG, WebP, AVIF).

LEARN AS YOU GO
Help & tutorials cover every tool, with guided tours that run in a practice scene: the editor points at each control and waits while you try it, and your own project stays safe.

DESIGN AGENT (OPTIONAL)
Describe what you want and watch it being drawn and animated, using your own API key from the AI provider you choose, or the AI assistant you already use in your browser.

Your projects are saved as .SVGcinema files and open straight in the app. The editor works offline once installed.
