01

Draw

Make the artwork, or bring it in. Tools stay active after each shape, the way they do in design apps.

{{CAP:editor-draw|The editor: the tool rail, the Layers panel with groups, masks and clip paths, and the canvas with the sample scene|shot}}

Every tool is one key away

Select V, pen P, rectangle R, text T, bones B, pins W. Letters go by key position, so they work on any keyboard layout. Snapping and guides keep things lined up, and the Layers panel shows groups, masks and clip paths as a tree.

Pen and pencil P

Click for a corner, drag for a curve, or draw freehand. Edit any node afterwards.

Shapes

Rectangles, ellipses, polygons, stars and lines. Sides, points and corner radius stay editable, and animate.

Text and fonts

PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP, AVIF, HEIC and more, resized and compressed on export.

Booleans and align

Union, subtract, intersect and exclude; align, distribute, flip and turn in one bar.

02

Style

Colour, strokes, effects and masks. Every setting here is a property, so every one of them can be keyed.

{{CAP:filters|The Filters section of the inspector: Add filter, a note that filters don’t animate in CSS exports, and a Drop shadow card with X, Y, Blur, Spread, Color and Opacity, each with its own keyframe diamond|shot}}

Filters you stack and animate

Blur, drop shadow with spread, glow, inner shadow and glow, colour adjustments, colour overlay and outlines. Each filter is a card: collapse it, drag it to reorder, and key any of its values.

{{ART:animated-gradients}}

Fill and gradients

Solid, linear or radial. Stops, angle, centre and radius all blend between keyframes.

{{ART:dash-patterns}}

Strokes

Width, line ends and joins, dash patterns that blend, and alignment: centre, inside or outside.

{{ART:masks-and-clipping}}

Masks and clipping

Soft masks by opacity or brightness, so a gradient wipe reveals as you’d expect.

03

Animate

Key anything in the inspector, then reach for the tools made for motion.

{{CAP:inspector-tracks|The inspector’s Transforms section: Position, Scale, Skew, Rotate and Origin, each with a keyframe diamond; Scale is animated|shot}}

A diamond beside every property

Click it to start a track. From then on, change the value anywhere on the timeline and a keyframe appears there. Grey is off, an outline is animated, filled means a key sits under the playhead.

{{ART:shape-morphing}}

Shape morphing

Any path into another; nodes are matched so the in-betweens stay clean.

{{ART:motion-paths}}

Motion paths

Any path becomes a route, followed at an even speed, turning with the curve.

{{ART:trim-path}}

Trim path Shift D

Lines that draw themselves on, and dashes that travel.

{{ART:bones-and-ik}}

Bones and IK B

Rig a stalk, an arm or a tail; pose it forward or by inverse kinematics, squash and stretch it.

{{ART:pins-and-mesh-warp}}

Pins and mesh warp W

Bend a path, an image, text or a whole group by its pins; pins can follow bones.

{{CAP:presets|The Presets window|crop crop-presets}}

Animation presets

Entrances, exits, emphasis, strokes, colour and filters, slotted in at the playhead.

04

Timing

Where an animation starts to feel right: when things happen, and how they get there.

{{CAP:easing|The Easing panel: In, Out and In-Out, the easing presets three across with Back picked, the curve editor, Use on whole animator and Delete key|narrow}}

The Easing panel

Select keyframes and press Easing: In, Out or In-Out, presets from Linear to Bounce, and a curve editor whose range fits overshooting curves like Back and Elastic. Save your own curve as a preset.

{{CAP:timeline|The timeline|crop crop-tl}}

The timeline

One row per property. Select layers and keys with Ctrl and Shift; keys snap to the playhead and to each other.

{{CAP:graph|The graph editor|crop crop-graph}}

Graph editor

Open a track into a value graph: drag keys for value and time, shape the easing with handles.

{{ART:onion-skin}}

Onion skin Alt O

Outlines of where the selected layers were and will be, before and after the playhead.

05

Interaction

How the animation plays once it’s on a page. You choose this when you export.

{{CAP:export-scroll|Export with Starts set to Linked to scroll: a test page with start and end lines and how much has played|shot}}

Linked to scroll

The page’s scroll becomes the playhead: scrolling down plays it, scrolling back plays it backwards. Pick when it plays, smooth the wheel steps, and try it on a test page right in the Export window.
