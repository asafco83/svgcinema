DESIGN AGENT

Describe it.
Watch it animate.

The agent works in your project with the editor’s own tools: it draws shapes and SVG artwork, edits layers, applies presets, sets keyframes and morphs. Every step lands on the canvas and the timeline, where you can change it by hand.

IMAGES AND VECTOR

Vector when it moves. Pictures when you ask.

  • ◆Drawn by the agent, free. Or a vector model (Recraft via OpenRouter, or your own service), whose SVG comes in as editable layers.
  • ◆Prefer vector when parts move. When a request doesn’t say, this switch decides; off, the agent asks on a card.
  • ◆Confirm before generating. Edit the prompt, see the model, size and price. Turn it off and each image shows its cost instead.
  • ◆Transparent backgrounds, even from models that can’t make one: the background is removed in your browser.
YOU STAY IN CHARGE

It works in the open.

While it works, editing pauses, but play, scrub and zoom still do. Its cursor shows where it is.

One request, one undo

Undo, or “Undo this request” under its answer, rolls the whole request back.

Stop keeps what’s done

Stop at any moment; the steps already taken stay, as one undo step.

A step cap you set

20 steps by default, 1 to 100. At the cap it pauses with what’s done and what’s left; Continue gives it that plus two.

Asks before deleting

Layers are never removed without a yes, unless you turn the question off.

Checks its work

Models that can see look at a frame and fix what’s wrong, up to three times a request.

Plain errors

What happened, whether anything changed, and Try again.

PRIVACY

No SVGcinema server in between.

The agent runs in your browser. Your key is kept there and goes straight to your provider. Chats are saved in the project file only if you tick Include AI agent chats when saving, and Settings › Privacy clears the chat or removes the keys.

QUESTIONS

Which model should I use?

One that can use tools. Test tells you, and whether it can see images, which lets it check its own frames.

What does it cost?

The agent is free. You pay your provider for what you use, and the model menu shows each price before you pick.

Can I use a model on my own computer?

Yes: choose Custom and give any OpenAI-compatible address. A local server may need no key; it must allow requests from the page.

Does OpenAI image generation need anything extra?

OpenAI may ask you to verify your organization before its image models work with your key.
