Animate on the timeline

Click the diamond beside a property to start animating it. Move the playhead, change the value, and a keyframe appears. Or describe the motion and let the agent key it.

  • Shift PAdd a position keyframe at the playhead
  • Shift OOpacity. Also Shift R rotate and Shift S scale

DESIGN AGENT

Describe it. It draws, keys and checks its own work.

Ask for “a cartoon rocket that launches” and the agent works in your project with the editor’s own tools: shapes, layers, presets, keyframes and shape morphs. You watch every step on the canvas.

  • ◆One request, one undo step. Roll back the whole request, or stop it half-way and keep what’s done.
  • ◆It looks at its work. Models that can see take a snapshot of the canvas and fix what’s off.
  • ◆It asks first. Before deleting layers, and before paying for an image, with the price.
  • ◆No middleman. Keys stay in your browser and go straight to your provider.

Every property gets its own track.

Color and opacity, a gradient’s angle, a blur’s radius, a star’s corner radius. If you can set it in the inspector, you can put a keyframe on it.

Shape morphing

Morph any path into another. SVGcinema splits and matches the nodes so the in-between frames stay clean.

Motion paths

Set any path as a motion path. The layer follows it at an even speed and can turn along the curve.

Trim path Shift D

Start, end and offset are all animatable, for lines that draw themselves on and dashes that travel.

Bones and IK B

Rig a stalk, an arm or a tail with bones. Pose it with forward or inverse kinematics, squash and stretch it, and key the whole rig in one go.

Pins and mesh warp W

Pin a path, an image, text or a whole group, then drag the pins to bend it. Pins can follow bones, so a rig bends a sleeve or a vine.

Filters

Blur, shadows and glows, color adjustments and outlines. Stack them on any layer, drag to reorder, and every setting has its own track.

Animation presets

Entrances, exits, emphasis, strokes, color and filters in one click, staggered across layers. They slot into the keyframes you already have.

Graph editor

Open any number track into a value graph: drag keys for value and time, shape the easing with handles.
