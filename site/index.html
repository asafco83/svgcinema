Every property gets its own track.

Colour and opacity, a gradient’s angle, a blur’s radius, a star’s corner radius. If you can set it in the inspector, you can put a keyframe on it.

Shape morphing

Morph any path into another. SVGcinema splits and matches the nodes so the in-between frames stay clean.

Motion paths

Set any path as a motion path. The layer follows it at an even speed and can turn along the curve.

Trim path Shift D

Start, end and offset are all animatable, for lines that draw themselves on and dashes that travel.

Bones and IK B

Rig a stalk, an arm or a tail with bones. Pose it with forward or inverse kinematics, squash and stretch it, and key the whole rig in one go.

Animated gradients

Linear and radial gradients blend their stops, angle, centre and radius. They can even fade in from a solid colour.

Masks and clipping

Soft masks work by opacity or brightness, so a gradient wipe reveals as you’d expect. Clipping paths use only the shape.

Custom easing

Click the line between two keyframes to pick a preset, or drag the handles to draw your own curve.

Blur and drop shadow

Stack filters on any layer and reorder them. Every parameter, from radius to shadow offset, has its own track.

Pins and mesh warp W

Pin a path, an image, text or a whole group, then drag the pins to bend it. Pins can follow bones, so a rig bends a sleeve or a vine.

Size and corners

Shapes grow and shrink around their origin while the stroke keeps its width. Corner radius on rectangles, polygons and stars animates too.

Dash patterns

Dash patterns blend from one keyframe to the next, even patterns of different lengths, and the offset sends them travelling.

Triggers and links

Play on load, on hover, on click, when scrolled into view or from your code, and pick what mouse-out does. Any layer can link to a page.

Animation presets

Entrances, exits, emphasis, strokes, colour and filters in one click, staggered across layers. They slot into the keyframes you already have.

Graph editor

Open any number track into a value graph: drag keys for value and time, shape the easing with handles.

Onion skin

See where the selected layers were and will be, as outlines before and after the playhead.

Symbols

Reuse artwork: every instance moves on its own, while edits to the shapes reach them all. Exported once, used many times.

Animate on the timeline

Click the diamond beside a property to start animating it. Move the playhead, change the value, and a keyframe appears.

  • Shift PAdd a position keyframe at the playhead
  • Shift OOpacity. Also Shift R rotate and Shift S scale
  • SpaceTap to play and pause, hold to pan

Bring photos. Ship them light.

Images are layers like any other: move, scale, rotate, fade and mask them alongside your vectors. On export, SVGcinema can shrink each one to the size it actually plays at.

Add images

Drop files on the canvas or into the Assets panel, then click one to place it. PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP, AVIF, BMP, ICO, TIFF and HEIC all work; TIFF and HEIC are converted for you. Your images are kept in this browser between visits and saved, untouched, inside the project file.

Optimize on export

Turn on Resize and compress for this size in the export dialog, or leave it off to embed the originals.

  • Sized to fit. Each image is scaled down to the largest size it reaches anywhere in the animation, including scale keyframes, at the export width you set.
  • Sharp on 2× screens. Optionally keeps twice that resolution for high-density displays.
  • Smallest format wins. Automatic mode tries AVIF and WebP where your browser can encode them, plus JPEG or PNG, and keeps the smallest. Or pick one format, or keep the original.
  • Quality you choose. A slider from 30% to 100% for lossy formats.
  • Safe by default. Transparent images never become JPEG, animated GIFs stay as they are, and an image that wouldn’t get smaller keeps its original file.

The export dialog lists every image with its size before and after, so you see the saving before you download.

One file. Two ways to play it.

What you see on the canvas is what ships: the editor draws your artwork with the same markup and the same player that go into the exported file.

Built-in player

A small dependency-free script inside the SVG. Everything animates.

  • Morphs, motion paths, bones, gradients, masks and every filter parameter
  • A player API to play, pause, seek, reverse and change speed, with events
  • Repeat, ping-pong, speed and frame rate, set in the export dialog

0:15.00

Open any SVG. Even animated ones.

Bring in work from other tools and keep editing it. Layers, shapes, text and keyframes come in as things you can select and change, not as a flat picture.

Figma, Illustrator, Inkscape

Groups keep their names and order, transforms become position, rotate and scale, shapes stay shapes and text stays text.

Animated SVGs

Animations exported from supported SVG animation tools, with a JS player or as CSS, open as keyframes you can edit: motion paths, easing, morphs, gradients, and the length, repeat and speed.

CSS animations

@keyframes on an SVG’s elements become keyframes too: opacity, colours, stroke, dashes, sizes and transforms.

Your own exports, editable

Tick Include the editable project when you export, and the SVG opens back in SVGcinema exactly as you left it.

Masks and filters

Clip paths and masks become clipping and mask groups; a blur or a drop shadow becomes a filter you can animate.

Open or add

Open an SVG as a new project, or add it to the one you’re in: its layers arrive in a group, and a longer animation lengthens your timeline.

Every tool is one key away.

Drawing tools stay active after each shape, the way they do in Adobe apps. Press a key on your keyboard to try it here.

Pick a key to see what the tool does.

Snapping and guides

Snap to other layers’ edges and centres, to path nodes, guides and the grid. Drag guides out of the rulers.

Align and combine

Align and distribute layers against each other or the canvas, and combine shapes with union, subtract, intersect and exclude.

Drag any number

Drag the label of a number field left or right to change the value. Every animatable property keeps its own row and its own keyframe toggle.

Free, and it stays on your machine.

SVGcinema is a static web app. Your artwork is drawn, animated and exported right in the browser tab.

No account

Open the editor and start drawing. There’s nothing to sign up for and no watermark on exports.

Projects are files

Save as a .SVGcinema file, a standard zip with your layers, keyframes and original images inside.

Picks up where you left off

The last project and its images are restored when you come back, from storage in your own browser.

Fonts handled

Google Fonts turn into outlines by default, or embed only the alphabets your text uses. Hebrew keeps its right-to-left order.

Your exports are yours

Use, share and sell the SVG files you export, commercially too. That includes the player inside them.

Light or dark

The editor follows your system theme until you pick one. This page shares that choice.
